SimpleHome Privacy Policy

Last updated September 27, 2026

SimpleHome is a personal app used by a single household to control its own devices.

What the app accesses

When you sign in with Google, SimpleHome requests access to the Nest devices you choose to share (scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/sdm.service). It uses this only to show your thermostat's temperature, mode and status, and to change them when you ask.

Where data is kept

Sign-in tokens are stored in the iOS Keychain on your iPhone. Your list of devices is stored on your iPhone and backed up to your own iCloud account. SimpleHome has no server: nothing is sent to the developer or any third party. Device commands go directly from your iPhone to Google's API.

Sharing and retention

SimpleHome does not sell, share or analyze your data. You can revoke access at any time at nestservices.google.com/partnerconnections or by signing out in the app; deleting the app removes its data from your iPhone.
